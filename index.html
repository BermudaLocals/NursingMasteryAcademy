Module 2: Diabetic & Dietary Logic

Understanding the "Highs and Lows" of insulin management.

The 15-15 Rule for Hypoglycemia:

  1. Give 15 grams of fast-acting carbs (juice/glucose tabs).
  2. Wait 15 minutes and re-check blood sugar.
  3. Repeat if glucose is still below 70 mg/dL.
